# UX review: Ayo Bicara (Speaking Game), learner game and admin

Panel: **Nadia** (14, SMP student, casual mobile gamer), **Kevin** (senior game UI designer), **Maya** (senior EdTech UX designer), **Pak Hendra** (English teacher who sets up questions). None of us has a disability.
Date: 2026-09-28. Build: the running app at `http://localhost:8000` (uvicorn --reload, Postgres :55432). We did not restart it.
Owner goal: make it look as appealing as possible ("semenarik mungkin"), with a game-console feel that makes learners want to come back.

## How we tested (observed, not inferred)
- We used Playwright Chromium 1228 (headless) with a fake mic: `--use-fake-device-for-media-stream --use-file-for-fake-audio-capture`. The WAVs came from macOS `say`, padded with 6 s of silence: "I like to eat rice" (3★), "I like to eat" (2★), "I like to" (1★), a wrong answer ("banana telephone purple window"), "good morning", "I see a cat on the sofa", "blue", and pure silence.
- We played at 1280 and 360 px, in dark and light themes. We covered all 4 modes, the 0/1/2/3★ results, a time-up, no speech, retry ×4, quitting mid-round, the summary, level-up, the home screen after a round, and forced errors (mic denied, attempt 500, leaderboard 500, a mode with no questions, slow load).
- Sessions return questions in random order. To get a known target we changed the `POST /api/sessions` response in the test browser, either moving a question to the front or putting in the public view of an active question. The backend accepted this (see UX-01, note 3).
- Admin (1280 + 360): wrong token, the list, creating an empty question (validation), a new read_aloud question plus Uji soal, a new quick_answer question with keywords, a CSV import (4 rows), stats, and archiving.
- Test users: UX Tester 1 (id 53) and UX Tester 2 (id 58). Test questions #74–#78 all start with `[UX]` and are **archived** (checked with the admin API, `is_active=false`).
- Scripts: `scratchpad/ux/{lib,s1,s2,s3}.mjs`. Screenshots: `reviews/shots/ux-*.png`.

## Scores (1–5)
| Persona | First impression | Fun / motivation | Clarity | Polish | Admin efficiency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nadia (student) | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | — |
| Kevin (game UI) | 4 | 3 | 4 | 3 | — |
| Maya (EdTech UX) | 4 | 2 | 3 | 4 | — |
| Pak Hendra (teacher) | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3 |

- **Nadia:** "The neon look is cool and the three-star pop with confetti feels great, really. But after one round I already knew I could just 'Coba lagi' the easy sentence to top the leaderboard. When I said nothing at all it still told me 'Hampir!'. Will I open it tomorrow? Only if there's something new or a daily streak. Right now there's nothing waiting for me."
- **Kevin:** "The juice is there: star pop, count-up, float-plus, combo flame, shake, synth SFX, a timer ring that turns red. The problem is the play screen. The cartridge colour identity disappears once you're in: the ring is always cyan, the button is always purple. And the reward loop gets exploited from the first minute. Quitting after one question gives you 'STAGE CLEAR' and an A. That breaks trust in every other reward."
- **Maya:** "Word-level colours show *what* went wrong, but a learner in Baca Nyaring can't *hear* the correct word. 0 % and 'no speech detected' get the same 'Hampir!' plus the fail sound. The leaderboard is all-time, lists 0-point classmates, and can be farmed, which hurts weak learners most. The copy tone is good for teens."
- **Pak Hendra:** "Uji soal is excellent. I typed 'docter' and saw 'doctor' marked *hampir* before saving. CSV import with a preview and per-row validation is excellent too. But my `elephant;tree` row imported as ONE keyword and was marked 'Siap'. No child could ever pass that question. The stats don't tell me which words my class misses, and on my phone the list is endless."

### Verdict on the builder's known issues
- **(a) Retry farming:** real, and bigger than "earns points again". Retries also extend the combo multiplier and multiply stars. Together with quit-early (UX-02) and a missing session/question check, the whole reward economy can be gamed in the first minute. This is a blocker for any leaderboard. Fix it with best-attempt-per-question scoring (UX-01).
- **(b) Generic history text:** real. It is worse on mobile (truncated), and worse because it lists attempts not rounds. It needs a one-line API change (`COALESCE(target_text, prompt)`) plus grouping by session (UX-09).
- **(c) Hard-coded difficulty labels:** real, and currently *wrong* for 2 of 4 modes. It is lower impact than (a)/(b) and fits the `/api/games` payload (UX-12).

## Top 5 changes that would most increase daily use
1. **Make points fair:** best-attempt-per-question scoring, streak over questions, no Stage Clear or rank on quit (UX-01, UX-02). Every other reward depends on this.
2. **Add a daily return loop:** daily streak "🔥 N hari", a daily 5-question challenge with 2× XP, level titles, and unlockable avatars/skins (UX-08).
3. **Make feedback teach:** tap any word to hear it, a model-sentence button in every mode, "kamu bilang *race*", and a post-round "latih kata tersulit" (UX-05, UX-14).
4. **Protect weak learners:** a weekly leaderboard without 0-point rows plus "you ±2", a no-speech state that doesn't punish, and honest 0 % copy (UX-07, UX-04).
5. **Make Jawab Cepat feel like a real arcade mode:** a 3-2-1 intro, a countdown from reveal with auto-mic, and a mode-coloured ring/orb on every play screen (UX-03, UX-13).

## Strengths to keep
- **Juice and timing are genuinely good.** Tap-to-record in about 95 ms. Stop-to-result median about 1.3 s with a clear "Menilai…" equaliser. Staggered star-pop with rising tones, a count-up, a floating "+N", a combo flame, a fail shake, confetti on 3★, a synth SFX set with a mute toggle, and a timer ring that turns red at 5 s. (ux-06, ux-07, ux-08, ux-36)
- **Home cartridges:** four colour-coded "cartridges" with a tag line, pips, count and a "MAIN ▶" pill. They read as a game menu at both 1280 and 360, with no horizontal scroll and the first cartridge above the fold. (ux-04, ux-23)
- **Copy tone** in Bahasa Indonesia fits teens ("Mantap jiwa! Tiga bintang!", "Ayo ngomong Inggris!"). The 3-step "Cara main" onboarding is short and skippable. (ux-02, ux-03)
- **Error handling is mostly thoughtful:** mic-denied copy tells you exactly what to do (ux-45), an empty mode shows "SEGERA" plus a friendly toast (ux-48), and there is a health banner for Vosk/DB.
- **Light theme is complete and pleasant** at 360 and 1280. (ux-40, ux-42, ux-43, ux-44)
- **Admin Uji soal** is a standout. It shows live scoring of a typed answer with per-word status, stars and points *before* saving (ux-54, ux-56). **CSV import** has a preview table, per-row validation, "Isi contoh", progress, and accepts Indonesian mode names (ux-57, ux-58). Empty-save validation is clear and per field (ux-53).
