# Accessibility & inclusive-design review: Speaking Game ("Ayo Bicara")

**Panel:** Rina (blind, screen reader), Budi (low vision), Sari (motor, keyboard/switch), Dimas (Deaf/HoH), Ayu (dyslexia/ADHD/anxiety), Yoga (speech impairment)
**Date:** 2026-09-28. **Build:** running instance at `http://localhost:8000`, Postgres :55432. Nothing was restarted.
**Method:** Playwright Chromium 1.57 (chromium-1228), with a fake mic fed by macOS `say` WAVs, so every round below was actually played. We ran axe-core (WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 A+AA + best-practice) on every screen in both themes, dumped the ARIA tree, walked the Tab order and recorded each stop's size and focus ring, and logged live-region mutations with a MutationObserver. Screens were captured at 1280 / 360 / 320 px, at 200% and 400% zoom, in light and dark themes, and with `prefers-reduced-motion`. Colour-blind views use a deuteranopia matrix and greyscale. Scoring for disfluent speech was probed with `POST /api/admin/preview-score`.
**Test users:** A11y Tester 1–5 (ids 52+). No demo users or questions were modified.
Scripts and raw JSON are in the session scratchpad (`scratchpad/a11y/*.mjs`, `*-out.json`). Screenshots are in `reviews/shots/a11y-*.png`.

---

## Verdicts: "Could I finish a game session alone?"

**Rina (blind, VoiceOver/NVDA): with difficulty. No for Ceritakan Gambar.**
Much of the structure is solid. There is a skip link, every screen change moves focus to its heading, the four game cards have full names ("Baca Nyaring… Tantangan Mudah. 7 soal tersedia."), English text is marked `lang="en"`, and each word result carries a hidden status such as "she (Terlewat)". axe found no violations on login, home, play or summary. Then I hit the mic. I pressed Enter on "Mulai rekam", the button disabled itself, and my focus fell to `<body>`. My next Enter did nothing and the recording kept running. When I pressed Tab I landed on the skip link at the top of the page (A11Y-01). I only finished rounds because the onboarding happens to mention Space. I am never told the time limit, and I get no warning before it runs out: the countdown is `aria-hidden`. The first thing I hear is "Waktu habis! Rekaman dikirim." (A11Y-06). In Ceritakan Gambar the whole question is an emoji announced only as "Gambar soal", so I cannot know it is a sun and a beach (A11Y-04).

**Budi (low vision, zoom, high contrast, dark mode): yes.**
The game's contrast is good. axe passed every learner screen in both themes, and the word chips I measured came out at 5.9–8.3:1 in dark mode. Reflow is clean: at 320 px, 200% and 400% there is no horizontal scroll (`scrollWidth == innerWidth` on every screen). What I can't read reliably is the word feedback. In greyscale "Tepat" and "Hampir" look identical, and so do their legend swatches (A11Y-07). The light-theme "Hampir" chip is 4.42:1. The admin back office fails contrast on 50 nodes (A11Y-11).

**Sari (tremor, keyboard/switch only): with difficulty.**
Recording is a toggle, not hold-to-talk. The mic target is huge (124 px) and every Tab stop has a visible cyan ring. Those are good choices. But Enter-to-start throws my focus away (A11Y-01). The Space shortcut is global, so pressing Space on "✕" (quit) or on "Dengarkan" starts a recording instead (A11Y-05). The worst part is the timer. With a tremor I need more than 10 s to start, speak and stop. The app cut me off at the limit and submitted a half answer that scored 0%, and I could not extend or turn off the timer (A11Y-02).

**Dimas (Deaf / hard of hearing): yes for 3 modes. No for Tirukan Aku.**
Every sound effect has a visual twin: start/stop changes the orb and the status text, and each result has text, stars and chips. The mute toggle is correctly `aria-pressed`. Tirukan Aku, though, is TTS only. The card literally says "Teksnya disembunyikan. Dengarkan sebanyak yang kamu mau." I have no caption, no reveal option and no visual alternative, and the home card doesn't warn me that the mode needs hearing (A11Y-03).

**Ayu (dyslexia, ADHD, anxiety): with difficulty.**
The copy is kind ("Belum pas, tapi jangan menyerah!"), onboarding is 3 short steps, and with reduced motion on, all confetti, shake and count-up animations stop (verified: `document.getAnimations()` was empty). With the default setting, though, the picture floats, the "hidden text" bars wave, the orb breathes and the XP bar shines forever while I am trying to concentrate. There is no in-app way to stop them (A11Y-10). The countdown ring turning red, the auto-submit, the letter grade "Rank C" and a leaderboard that is always visible all add stress (A11Y-02, A11Y-20). Strikethrough on missed words makes them harder for me to read (A11Y-07).

**Yoga (stutter / dysarthria): with difficulty. Close to no on the 10-second modes.**
The good news: I sent a transcript with repetitions ("the the the cat is is on the table") through preview-score and still got 100%, 3 stars. Blocks and repetitions don't cost accuracy. The bad news: Tirukan Aku and Jawab Cepat give me 10 s, and the app cut me off mid-sentence and auto-submitted (observed: auto-stop at the limit, 0% result). My retries count against me. The summary averages every attempt ("6 percobaan", 9% average), and a failed attempt resets the combo (A11Y-08). The result screen then lists my repetitions back to me as "Kata tambahan: the, the, is" (A11Y-19). There is no accommodation mode: no relaxed timer, no "unclear counts as correct", no opt-out of the speed bonus (A11Y-09).

Notes on the known issues we were asked to check:
- **20 s countdown auto-stops recording (2.2.1):** confirmed, and it is worse than described. Limits are 10–25 s per question, and the 10 s modes (Tirukan Aku, Jawab Cepat) are the harshest (A11Y-02).
- **repeat_after_me is TTS-only:** confirmed. There is no text, caption or reveal path before answering (A11Y-03).
- **Feedback relies on word colours plus strikethrough:** partly confirmed. Missed words (strikethrough) and unclear words (dashed border) do have non-colour cues, but correct vs close is colour-only, the legend is colour-only, and screen-reader text is present (A11Y-07).

## Strengths to keep (don't regress these)

- **Scoring tolerates disfluency.** Word repetitions and fillers ("um", "uh") don't lower accuracy (preview-score, 100%). Keep `SequenceMatcher` alignment crediting target words only.
- **Toggle to record, not hold-to-talk.** The mic button is 124×124 px.
- **Visible focus ring on every Tab stop**, walked on login, home, all 4 play modes, result, admin list and editor. `:focus-visible` uses a 3 px offset ring.
- **Skip link**, `<html lang="id">` with `lang="en"` on every English sentence, transcript and word list.
- **Focus management on screen change:** it moves to the screen's heading (`show()`, `game.js:244-247`). After a result it goes to "Lanjut", and after Retry/Next it goes to "Soal n / 5".
- **Screen-reader extras:** each word chip has hidden "(Tepat/Hampir/…)". Keyword chips have ✓/✗ plus "(disebut/belum disebut)". The sr-only result sentence ("Hasil: 0 bintang, 20 poin, akurasi 20%.") and the rank `img` label are good. Game cards have complete `aria-label`s.
- **axe-core:** 0 violations on login, onboarding, home, all 4 play screens and summary, in both dark and light themes.
- **Contrast in the learner game** passes AA in both themes. The dark word chips measure 5.9–8.3:1.
- **Reflow:** no horizontal scroll at 320 px, 360 px, 200% or 400% zoom, for learner and admin.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured completely:** CSS loops, confetti canvas, shake, count-up and float "+N" are all disabled.
- **Onboarding dialog:** native `<dialog>`, focus goes to "Lanjut", Esc closes it and focus returns to the heading, and there is an sr-only "Langkah 1 dari 3".
- **Sound is never the only channel** (apart from Tirukan Aku). There is a mute toggle with `aria-pressed` and a changing label.
- **Kind, plain-Bahasa copy** for failure and mic errors ("Izin mikrofon ditolak. Klik ikon gembok…").
- **Admin:** real `<table>` with column headers, action buttons named per row ("Edit soal #25"), mode filter as a `group` of `aria-pressed` buttons, focus moves to the first invalid field.
